w

Designed for discovery. Ready for action. Useful descriptions, accurate page titles, working links, and labeled actions come built in to support discovery and citations in AI answers, and help browsing agents navigate your pages. You don't need to learn the mechanics.

01 / ACCESS

Can they reach it?

Webby checks whether the page responds and what its access instructions tell search and AI services about visiting and indexing it.

02 / MEANING

Can they read it?

It looks for useful visible text, a clear page title and description, meaningful headings, and links that point somewhere real.

03 / ACTION

Can they use it?

It checks named links, buttons, and fields, captures pages at real screen sizes, and samples keyboard access. Completing a full task, such as submitting a form, needs a targeted browser check.

Built into the draft. For a live site, ask for evidence and a specific fix.
