03 / FINDABILITYTHE QUIET HALF OF GREAT DESIGN

Beautiful
is only half
the job.

✳

Your next visitor might use a browser, a search result, or an AI agent. Webby inspects the live page’s access rules, visible content, and usable actions.

01 / ACCESS

Can they reach it?

Webby checks the page response and the signals that tell search and AI services whether they may visit.

02 / MEANING

Can they read it?

It looks for useful visible text, clear page information, and links that point somewhere real.

03 / ACTION

Can they use it?

It checks labels on links, buttons, and form fields in the page HTML, then flags browser tasks for manual review.

Evidence from the page. A specific fix. No mystery score.
